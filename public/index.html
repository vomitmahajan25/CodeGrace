Complete the organization eligibility screen, receive an access code immediately if you qualify, verify assigned documents, record source-backed evidence, and disburse loans assigned to you.

◎03 / LOANOFFICER

The single configured LoanOfficer reviews customer documents and SHAP explanations, approves or rejects applications, assigns the funding lender, and tracks all customer and lender activity.

  • Customer and application directory
  • SHAP-backed human decisions
  • Lender performance and disbursement totals

RESPONSIBLE BY DESIGN

A score should start a conversation — not end one.

Trustcred is an early-stage credit-assessment platform. The current score formula is not a validated credit-risk model and must not be used alone to approve loans. Human review, informed consent, data security, and applicable lending requirements remain essential.
