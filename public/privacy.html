PRIVACY AND DATA HANDLING

Trustcred Privacy Notice

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Before accepting live customers, the Trustcred operator must publish its legal name, privacy/support contact, applicable retention schedule, and any jurisdiction-specific notices. This page describes the current application behavior; it is not a substitute for legal review.

1. Information processed

Trustcred processes account details including name, email address, account role, password hash, lender eligibility-screen submissions, access-code hashes, password-reset codes and session records. A customer application may include phone number, city, work or business history, self-reported income, requested loan amount, repayment tenure, and loan purpose. Customers can upload identity proof, address proof, bank statements, income/business proof, and utility bills. The lender may record financial metrics extracted from reviewed documents, notes explaining the source evidence, and an assessment index.

2. Purposes

Information is used to operate and secure accounts, verify email addresses, assess application evidence, route cases to an assigned lender, record lender evidence assessments and final LoanOfficer decisions, answer support requests, and preserve an audit trail of important actions.

3. Who can access records

Customers can access their own applications and submitted documents. A lender can access applications assigned to that lender. The LoanOfficer can oversee applications, lender access, customer verification, SHAP explanations, and recorded decisions. The service hosting the website, database, private document storage, and transactional email may process data to provide those services. Access is not intended to be public.

4. Documents and storage

Uploaded documents are stored in private object storage and retrieved through short-lived signed links. Application records and assessment fields are stored in the service database. The current application does not automatically connect to a bank, utility provider, or credit bureau; a lender manually reviews documents and enters the source metrics used in the evidence assessment.

5. Passwords and sessions

Passwords are stored as salted PBKDF2-derived hashes rather than plain text. Login uses an HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite session cookie with a 12-hour expiry. Email verification and password-reset codes are time-limited and stored as hashes. Lender access codes are issued by the LoanOfficer and stored as hashes.

6. Retention and deletion

The operator must establish and publish a retention schedule before using the service for real loan applications. In the current build, application, audit and assessment records can remain in the database until an authorized operator removes them; uploaded files also require an explicit deletion/retention process. Do not upload real identity or financial records until that schedule, deletion workflow, access review, backup policy, and incident-response procedure are in place.

7. Accuracy, consent and scoring

Customers are asked to confirm consent before submitting an application. They should provide accurate information and can ask the operator to correct inaccurate records. The Trustcred Evidence Index is an early-stage, rule-based estimate made from lender-entered evidence metrics. It is not a credit-bureau score and has not been validated against actual repayment outcomes. It should not be used as the sole basis for a lending decision.

8. Security and contact

The operator should publish a named privacy/support contact here before inviting customers and should provide a process to request access, correction, deletion, or review of information. Suspected account compromise or document exposure should be escalated promptly to that contact and the hosting provider.

Operator action required before real launch: add the operator’s legal identity and privacy email; set retention/deletion rules; complete a jurisdiction-specific privacy and lending compliance review.
