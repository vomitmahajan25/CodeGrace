Trustcred Terms of Use

Last updated: 9 October 2026

These initial terms require review and completion by the Trustcred operator and legal counsel before the service is used to process real credit applications.

1. Accounts and roles

Customers register with email and password and can use the service without email verification. Lenders submit their organization type, registration/license number, business address and phone, operating history, and declared annual lending capacity. Trustcred's initial eligibility screen requires at least two years of operation, a registration/license ID, reachable business contact details, and declared annual lending capacity of at least ₹5,00,000, plus an attestation that the organization is legally registered and authorized to lend. Applicants who pass receive a lender access code immediately and must enter it at every lender sign-in. This automated screen checks entered information and format only; it does not independently validate licensing, identity, solvency, or regulatory status. The LoanOfficer can rotate or revoke lender codes and makes final lending workflow decisions. The service supports a single LoanOfficer account configured by the operator. Users must keep passwords and lender access codes confidential and notify the operator if they suspect unauthorized access.

2. Accurate information and permissions

Users must provide truthful information and must only upload documents they are authorized to share. Lenders should access only applications assigned to them, verify documents individually, record reasons for corrections, and provide evidence-based recommendations. The LoanOfficer reviews the customer verification and saved SHAP explanation, then records the final application decision and rationale. Users must not attempt to access another customer's records, evade access controls, or upload malicious files.

3. Evidence assessment

The Evidence Index currently uses a disclosed, rule-based formula applied to metrics entered by a lender after source documents are reviewed. It is not CIBIL, is not a trained or independently validated credit-risk model, does not pull account data directly from financial providers, and does not constitute an approval, guarantee, offer of credit, or financial advice. The estimate must not be the sole basis for a real lending decision.

4. Availability and decisions

Trustcred provides application management, source-evidence review, and an explicit formula with Shapley/SHAP-style explanations. The current Evidence Index is not trained or validated against real repayment outcomes or an external credit-bureau score, and must not be the sole basis for lending. Service availability, third-party integrations, legal compliance, and model performance are not guaranteed. The operator must validate an appropriate credit-risk model on representative, consented, outcome-labelled data and meet applicable lending, privacy, fairness, and consumer-protection obligations before live lending decisions rely on automated risk estimates.

5. Sensitive information

Account, application, financial and uploaded document information is sensitive. The operator must implement and maintain appropriate access management, retention/deletion rules, incident handling, privacy disclosures, backups, and applicable legal requirements before live use. Users should not submit information they do not have permission to share.

6. Suspension and termination

The LoanOfficer may revoke lender access codes and disable access where necessary. The operator may restrict accounts or suspend the service for security, legal, maintenance or misuse reasons. The operator must provide an appropriate process for account closure and record deletion consistent with applicable retention obligations.

7. Changes and contact

The operator should identify the contracting entity, governing terms, effective date, and official support/contact channel before inviting customers. Continued use after a published update is subject to the revised terms as permitted by applicable law.

Launch checklist: complete the operator/legal details, privacy contact, complaints and correction process, retention schedule, lender operating policy, and independent review of scoring and lending compliance.
